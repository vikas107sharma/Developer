━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 📖 Common English Verbs & Their Meanings ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Everyday verbs that change meaning with context and prepositions.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🟢 GO ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔹 Change in state (transforms)
Milk went bad.

🔹 Progressing
How is work going?

🔹 Time passing
The meeting went by quickly.

🔹 Match / belong
These shoes go well with that dress.

🔹 Idioms
Can I ask you a question? — Yes, go ahead.
Go easy on him.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 👀 LOOK ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔹 Look (get attention)
Look, I need to tell you something serious.

🔹 Look like (appearance / similarity)
🔹 Look like (prediction / situation)
Looks like we are getting late.
Looks like it's going to rain.

🔹 Look into (research, investigate)
There is an issue. I will look into it.

🔹 Look after (take care, responsibility)
Can you look after my bag while I go to the bathroom?

🔹 Look forward to (excited for the future)
I am looking forward to seeing her.
📧 Emails: I am looking forward to hearing from you.

🔹 Look up (search for information)
I don't know the answer — let me look it up.

🔹 Look out (danger)
Look out for the slippery floor.
Watch out for danger.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🟡 GET ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔹 Receive something
I got a text message from my boss.

🔹 Buy
I am going to get a coffee.
Can you get me some bread from the store?

🔹 Become (change happening)
It's getting cold outside.
I am getting hungry.

🔹 Understand
Do you get what I am saying?

🔹 Arrive
We got home really late last night.

🔹 Arrange / make someone do something
I need to get this done.
Can you get a mechanic to help us?

🔹 get + adjective (change in state or feeling)
Get ready, get dressed, get lost, get started.

🔹 Get up (rise from bed / recover)
🔹 Get over (recover from something)
I got over the cold.
🔹 Get along (have a good relationship)
Do you get along with your co-worker?
🔹 Get back (return)
At what time will you get back home?
🔹 Get on / off
Get on the bus. Get off the bus.
🔹 Get through (survive something hard / finish it)
I can't believe I got through that exam.

📝 Story: I got up late, I got dressed quickly, I got on the bus, I got to
the office, I got my coffee, I got through all my meetings. I got tired,
I got home, and I got into bed.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🔵 KEEP ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔹 Have / not give away
You can keep the change.

🔹 Continue something
Keep walking. Keep practicing.

🔹 Store something
I keep my phone charger next to my bed.

🔹 Maintain a state
Keep calm. Keep quiet. Keep it clean.

🔹 Prevent something
Keep the kids away from the road.

🔹 Honour a promise / appointment
She always keeps her promises.

🔹 Records
The company keeps records of everything.

🔹 Keep someone waiting
Sorry, I kept you waiting.
Don't keep me waiting.

🔹 Keep up (match someone's pace)
I can't keep up with you — can you speak slower?

🔹 Keep an eye on (watch / monitor)
Can you keep an eye on the kids?


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🟣 MAKE ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔹 Create / produce
He made a video.
Can you make some tea?

🔹 Cause something to happen
The movie made me cry.

🔹 Force someone (no choice)
My mom made me eat vegetables.
My boss made me work late tonight.

🔹 Earn money
She makes $50 an hour.

🔹 Make it (arrive / manage to attend)
I can't make it to the party.

🔹 Make a decision
I made a decision to leave this place.

🔹 Make sense (logical / understandable)
Does this make sense? (= Do you understand?)


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🔴 TAKE ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔹 Pick up physically
She took the key from the counter.

🔹 Travel by something
I take the bus to work every morning.

🔹 Take time (require time)
Healing after a bad experience takes time.
Becoming good at anything takes time.
Starting a new job always takes time to get comfortable.

🔹 Take a look
Let me take a look at your code.

🔹 Take a photo
Can you take a picture of me in front of this mountain?

🔹 Take care of it (handle it — don't worry)
I will take care of it.

🔹 Take a break (pause, then continue)
🔹 Take a risk
🔹 Take it easy (relax, don't stress)

🔹 Take for granted (stop appreciating something valuable)
We often take the people we love for granted.
